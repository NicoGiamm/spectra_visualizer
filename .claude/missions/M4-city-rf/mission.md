# M4 — The city comes alive — radio & microwave ecosystem

## Problem

The city was nearly empty in radio and microwave; real cities are dense with Wi-Fi, cellular, Bluetooth, broadcast, trams and multipath in the street canyon. (Wi-Fi 2.4/5 GHz and cellular are microwave here; true radio > 1 m is FM/DAB, VHF airband, power lines, tram/LED interference — both bands get filled.)

## Scope

- In: emitter crowds, multipath bouncing, microwave haze, true radio, more visible/IR life, tuning.
- Out: city surfaces (M1/T6), the city audit group (M2/T3).

## Delivery

`spectra timeline city` 0 warnings; draw calls < ~400; shots along the walk with `--bands micro,radio` and `--bands ir`; physics-auditor on every new process.

## Current state

T1–T5 implemented on main in a9adb2f before missions existed (draw calls 82/211/213, 0 warnings reported), never reviewed or ticked: each is `needs-verification`. 6604214 fixed a falsified TETRA frequency found in review. T6 tuning partly done.
