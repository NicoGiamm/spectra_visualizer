# M1 — Spectral surfaces — the landscape shows the spectrum

## Problem

Only discrete emitters glowed; surfaces were lit in visible light or turned flat dark in instrument view. The sea should glow in IR, sand and rock cool, buildings leak heat, snow reflect UV, granite speckle in gamma, the sea look cold in microwave.

## Scope

- In: the surface property model and shader chunk, meter activity from surfaces, applying it to trail, seaside and city.
- Out: new events (other missions).

## Delivery

City surfaces (T6) ship with: `spectra timeline city` 0 warnings, draw calls within the < ~400 budget, shots at 5/30/54 + `--bands ir,micro`; the shared chunk must not change trail/seaside shots.

## Current state

T1–T5 done (trail and seaside verified). Open: T6, the city (per-building Tcore landed in 519656e; the rest to verify).

## Key decisions

- 2026-11-21 · Surface overlay with Visible on: a faint fixed `OVERLAY = .12` (`render/surface.js`), no HUD slider yet — plan Q3 as implemented.
